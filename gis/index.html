Featured project

PNW Wildfire Risk & Community Vulnerability Explorer

An open-source pipeline and interactive map that combines U.S. Forest Service wildfire hazard data with the CDC Social Vulnerability Index to score all 994 Oregon census tracts. It shows where high wildfire hazard and high social vulnerability coincide, with facility access and fire history for each tract.

I built it in 2026 working with an AI coding agent: I set the requirements, made the design decisions, and reviewed and tested the output. The methods, the limitations and all 30 design decisions are documented in the repository.
